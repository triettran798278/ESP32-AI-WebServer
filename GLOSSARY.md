# Apartment control demo

The demo groups independently controlled appliances by area.

## Language

**Area**

A zone presented separately in the apartment, such as the kitchen or a bedroom. Two areas can share a controller.

**Area device**

The ThingsBoard device record for a controller and the areas it serves.

**Appliance**

A physical load in an area, such as a lamp or fan. An area can contain multiple appliances.

**Relay channel**

An independently switched output on the area controller that powers an appliance.
